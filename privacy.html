Privacy policy

Smart FD Tracker. Last updated: 14 October 2026

What this app does

Smart FD Tracker helps you record your fixed deposits and track maturities, interest and tax estimates for you and your family.

Information stored on your phone

Only what you enter: profile names, relationship, senior citizen status and optional PAN, address, email, mobile number and tax details; and FD details such as bank, FD number, amounts, dates, nominee name and notes. This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone. The app has no server of its own and does not send this information to us.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Only when you tap Back up or Restore, the app signs in to your Google account and saves or reads one backup file in a private app folder of your own Google Drive. We cannot see this file. Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to Google Drive. You can delete the backup from your Google account settings (Manage app data).

Permissions

Notifications: to show maturity, interest payout, Form 121 and advance tax reminders. Screen lock / biometrics: for the optional App lock; Android checks your fingerprint, face or PIN and the app never sees it. Internet: to download the latest FD interest rates (a public rates file; nothing about you is sent), for the optional Google Drive backup, and for opening links such as email.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no selling or sharing of your information, and no account with us.

Payments

If you buy Premium, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or payment details.

Estimates, not advice

Interest, maturity and tax figures are estimates based on what you enter. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. Please confirm with your bank or a tax professional.

Your control

You can edit or delete any profile or FD in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your phone. A Google Drive backup stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that collect new information (for example, login with a mobile number), we will update this policy and ask for your consent before collecting it.

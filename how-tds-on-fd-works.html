How TDS on FD works

By Chaitanya Mankar · Updated 15 October 2026 · 6 min read

TDS (tax deducted at source) means your bank deducts tax from your FD interest and pays it to the government on your behalf. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, the rule is in section 393 (earlier, section 194A).

When does TDS apply?

The bank deducts TDS only once the interest it pays you in a year crosses a limit:

WhoLimit for each bank, each year
Residents below 60₹50,000
Senior citizens (60 and above)₹1,00,000
  • The limit counts the total interest on all your FDs at that bank (all branches), not each FD.
  • Each bank counts its own interest. Interest at different banks is not added together.
  • For cumulative FDs, the bank usually deducts TDS on the interest it credits for the year, even though you receive the money at maturity.

The rate

  • 10% when your PAN is on record.
  • 20% when PAN is missing or not valid.
  • NRIs have different rules (for example, NRO deposits attract a higher rate). This article covers residents.

Example: a resident below 60 earns ₹60,000 of interest at one bank. This is over the ₹50,000 limit, so the bank deducts 10%, which is ₹6,000, and you receive ₹54,000.

TDS is not the final tax

TDS is an advance payment of your tax. Your FD interest is added to your total income and taxed at your slab rate. If your tax is more than the TDS, you pay the difference (see advance tax for FD interest). If it is less, you claim a refund when you file your return.

Check that the TDS is credited

Your bank gives you a TDS certificate. Match it with the tax credit statement (Form 26AS) and the Annual Information Statement (AIS) on the income tax portal. Interest that appears there but is missing from your return can lead to a notice.

Ways to avoid unnecessary TDS

  • If your total tax for the year is nil, submit Form 121 to each bank before the interest is credited.
  • Keep your PAN linked with every bank, so the rate is 10%, not 20%.
  • Spread FDs across family members, since each person has their own limit and tax slabs. Gifts to family may have tax rules of their own, so ask a professional.

How the app shows TDS

In Smart FD Tracker, each profile has a TDS setting. Where TDS is deducted, the app shows 10% of the interest on the FD, in Interest and TDS by profile, in the interest certificate and on the renewal screen, where the new principal is adjusted. These are estimates, and your bank’s figures are the final word.

This article is general information based on rules published for the Income-tax Act, 2025 (in force from 1 April 2026). It is not tax advice. Your bank and a qualified tax professional have the final word for your situation.

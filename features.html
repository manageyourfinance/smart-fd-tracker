Features

Everything Smart FD Tracker does, screen by screen.

HomeSee your invested value and what it is worth today in one place. The amount rolls up smoothly when it changes.
MaturitiesA clear list of every FD and its maturity date, with the amount you will receive. Open any FD for the full detail, including payout schedule for non-cumulative FDs.
Add an FDPick the bank, enter the amount, rate, start date and tenure. While you type, the app shows the interest and the maturity amount, and for payout FDs the monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly payout.
Interest and TDSChoose a profile and see interest and TDS for the year. TDS is worked out per profile and shown wherever it applies, including the renewal screen.
Auto renewalIf an FD auto-renews, the app asks you to check the new principal and rate, so a renewal never goes unnoticed.
RemindersMaturity, interest payout on the payout day, Form 121 and advance tax. Each reminder is a short, plain notification.
Interest certificateA PDF interest certificate for any profile and tax year, with the interest earned on each FD and the TDS, ready to share or keep.
Prefilled Form 121The Form 121 list shows which profiles can submit it and which cannot. For each bank, the app generates a prefilled Part A draft with your details, that bank’s FDs and the estimated interest. Review it, then give it to the bank.
Advance tax alertsThe app estimates advance tax for each profile from the FDs you have entered, and reminds you 7 days and 1 day before each due date and on the day, with about how much to pay. Mark it paid in Reports.
Rate checkCompare your FD’s rate with current rates from major banks, including senior citizen rates. Rates come from banks’ published pages and are reviewed before they are released to the app.
LanguagesEnglish, Hindi and Marathi, switch any time.
Backup and lockOptional backup to your own Google Drive, and optional app lock with fingerprint, face or PIN.

What it keeps track of

MaturitiesEvery FD with its bank, amount, rate and maturity date, in one list. See what matures this month, this quarter or this year.
Interest and TDSInterest by profile and financial year, with TDS worked out for each profile. Where TDS applies, the app shows the 10% deduction and adjusts the renewal amount.
RemindersMaturity, interest payout, Form 121 and advance tax reminders, a simple notification on the day. Tap it and the app opens the right screen.
ReportsPDF interest certificates and Form 121 lists you can share or keep, for the profile and year you pick.
RatesCompare your FD’s rate with current bank rates before it matures, so you can decide whether to renew. (Premium)

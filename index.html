Smart FD Tracker

Keep every fixed deposit, maturity, interest and tax detail of your family in one place.

What it does

  • Profiles for each family member, with senior citizen status and TDS settings
  • Maturity reminders, interest payout reminders, Form 15G/15H (Form 121) and advance tax reminders
  • Interest and TDS reports by profile and financial year, with PDF export
  • Home screen with invested and current value
  • Bank FD rate comparison (Premium)
  • English, Hindi and Marathi
  • Optional app lock and optional Google Drive backup

Your data stays with you

Your profiles and FDs are stored only on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no account to create. See the privacy policy.

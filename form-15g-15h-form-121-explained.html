Form 15G vs Form 15H explained (Form 121 now)

By Chaitanya Mankar · Updated 15 October 2026 · 7 min read

If you have FD interest and your total tax is nil, you can ask the bank not to deduct TDS. For years that meant Form 15G or Form 15H. From 1 April 2026, under the Income-tax Act, 2025, both are replaced by a single Form 121.

The old forms

Form 15GForm 15H
WhoResidents below 60Residents aged 60 or above
Interest limitInterest within the basic exemption limitNo cap on the interest, but your tax must be nil

Form 121: one form for everyone

Form 121 is a self-declaration to the payer (for FDs, your bank) that no tax should be deducted because your total tax for the year will be nil. It sits under section 393 of the new Act.

  • Who can submit: resident individuals and HUFs. NRIs and companies cannot.
  • Condition: your estimated tax on total income for the tax year must be nil.
  • Below 60: the specified income you declare (such as interest) must stay within the basic exemption limit.
  • Aged 60 or above: you can submit it even if your interest is above that limit, as long as your tax is nil.
  • PAN: a valid PAN is mandatory. Without it, the declaration is invalid and the bank can deduct tax at a higher rate.

Form 15H submission: what senior citizens do now

If you used to submit Form 15H, you now submit Form 121 instead. The idea is the same. The bank checks your declaration and gives it a reference number, and you can submit it at the branch or through net banking or the bank’s app, depending on the bank.

Rules that trip people up

  • One form for each bank. With FDs at three banks, you submit three declarations. Each lists your other Form 121s for the same year.
  • Every tax year. It is valid for one tax year, so submit a new one each April.
  • Before the interest is credited. Submitting after TDS has been deducted does not undo it.
  • It is not a refund tool. If TDS was already deducted, claim it back in your income tax return.
  • It does not make income tax-free. If your total income is taxable, do not submit it. A wrong declaration can attract a penalty.

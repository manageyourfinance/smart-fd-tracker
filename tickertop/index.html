Questions

Does Ticker Top show Indian stocks like Nifty or Reliance?

Not yet. Showing NSE or BSE prices in an app needs a paid exchange licence. Ticker Top shows crypto, gold and forex from free sources today, and we plan to add Indian stocks later.

Will it drain my battery?

No. The strip is drawn very efficiently, prices refresh every 15 to 60 seconds (your choice), and nothing runs while the screen is off or every market on your list is closed.

Why does it ask for Display over other apps?

That permission lets the strip show on top of other apps. It cannot read or tap anything in those apps.

Where do the prices come from?

Crypto and gold from Binance public market data, with Coinbase as backup, and currency rates from Frankfurter, which publishes central-bank reference rates. Gold is shown through PAXG, a token backed by real gold.

Is this investment advice?

No. Prices are for information only and may be delayed. Ticker Top never tells you to buy or sell anything.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Open Google Play, go to Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Ticker Top. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Settings, Delete account, or see how to delete your account.

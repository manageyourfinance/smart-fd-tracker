Questions

Does Ticker Top show Indian stocks like Nifty or Reliance?

Not yet. Showing NSE/BSE prices in an app needs a paid exchange licence. Ticker Top shows crypto, gold and forex from free, licensed sources today, and we plan to add Indian stocks later.

Will it drain my battery?

No. The strip is drawn very efficiently, prices refresh only every 15–60 seconds (your choice), and nothing runs while the screen is off or every market on your list is closed.

Why does it ask for "Display over other apps"?

That permission lets the strip show on top of other apps. It can't read or tap anything in those apps.

Where do the prices come from?

Crypto and gold from Binance public market data (Coinbase as backup), and currency rates from Frankfurter, which publishes central-bank reference rates. Gold is shown via PAXG, a token backed by real gold.

Is this investment advice?

No. Prices are for information only and may be delayed. Ticker Top never tells you to buy or sell anything.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Settings → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.

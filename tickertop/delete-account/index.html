Delete your account

Ticker Top lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app

Open Ticker Top and go to Settings. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. Confirm, then choose your Google account when Google asks. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, write to managemyfinance.in@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Ticker Top account”. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Write an email

What gets deleted

Your Ticker Top account: name, email address and Firebase user ID. Your list and settings are only on your phone; uninstall the app to remove them.

What we keep

The start date of your free trial, linked to your email, so the free trial cannot be repeated. Purchase records are kept by Google Play, not by us, as required by tax and payment laws.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. To stop being charged, open Google Play, go to Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Ticker Top.

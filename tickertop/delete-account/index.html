Delete your Ticker Top account

Ticker Top (Android app by ManageMyFinance) lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Ticker Top and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm, then choose your Google account when Google asks. Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, email managemyfinance.in@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Ticker Top account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your Ticker Top account: name, email address and Firebase user ID.
  • Your list and settings are on your phone only. Uninstall the app to remove them.

What we keep

  • The start date of your free trial, linked to your email, kept indefinitely so the free trial can't be repeated.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play, not by us, as required by tax and payment laws.
Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Ticker Top.

Privacy policy

Ticker Top (Android app, com.managemyfinance.ticker). Last updated: 21 November 2026

What this app does

Ticker Top shows live crypto, gold and currency prices on a thin strip at the top of your phone’s screen, on top of other apps.

Who we are

Ticker Top is made by Chaitanya Mankar under the ManageMyFinance brand (managemyfinance.in), Pune, India.

Google sign-in

You sign in with your Google account so your free trial and plan stay with you. Firebase Authentication gives us your name, email address and a Firebase user ID. We never see your Google password.

Free trial record

The date and time your 7-day free trial started is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your email address, so the free trial can be used once per Google account.

Information stored on your phone

The coins, gold and currency pairs you add, their order, your country and currency, and your settings such as theme, speed and refresh time. This stays on your phone and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Price data

To show prices, the app asks public market-data services for the symbols on your list: Binance (data-api.binance.vision), Coinbase (api.exchange.coinbase.com) and Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). These requests contain only the symbols, for example BTC or USD/INR, never your name or email. Like any website, these services can see your device’s IP address, and their own privacy policies apply to it.

Permissions

Display over other apps: to show the ticker strip at the top of your screen. It cannot read or tap anything in other apps. Usage access (optional): only if you turn off Show on home screen, to check on your phone whether an app or the home screen is in front; this is never stored or sent anywhere. Internet: to fetch prices and to sign in. Foreground service: keeps the strip running while you use other apps.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking, no profiling, and no selling or sharing of your information with advertisers. Your information is processed only by Google (Firebase and Google Play) acting as our service provider, under Google’s privacy policy.

Payments

If you buy Ticker Top Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. The app only learns from Google whether Pro is active on your account.

Retention and deletion

Your account stays until you delete it. Delete it anytime in the app under Settings, Delete account, or follow the steps on the delete account page. We then remove your Firebase account (name, email and user ID). We keep only the start date of your free trial, linked to your email, so the trial cannot be repeated. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by Google Firebase. Your trial record can be read only by your own signed-in account.

Children

Ticker Top is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Prices, not advice

Prices may be delayed and are for information only. They are not financial or investment advice, and Ticker Top never tells you to buy or sell anything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: managemyfinance.in@gmail.com

Privacy policy

Ticker Top (Android app, package com.managemyfinance.ticker) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: you sign in with Google so your free trial and plan stay with your account. We store your Google account ID, your email and the date your free trial started. Your list of coins and currencies and your settings stay on your phone. We don't sell data, show ads or track you.

Who we are

Ticker Top is made by Chaitanya Mankar under the ManageMyFinance brand (managemyfinance.in), Pune, India. Contact: managemyfinance.in@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication): your name, email address and a Firebase user ID. We never see your Google password.
  • Free trial record (Cloud Firestore): the date and time your 7-day free trial started, stored under your email address.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing): payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. The app only receives from Google whether Ticker Top Pro is active on your account.

Information that stays on your phone

The coins, gold and currency pairs you add, their order, your country and currency, and your settings (theme, speed, refresh time and so on). Uninstalling the app removes them.

Price data

To show prices, the app asks public market-data services for the symbols on your list: Binance (data-api.binance.vision), Coinbase (api.exchange.coinbase.com) and Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). These requests contain only the symbols, for example BTC or USD/INR, never your name or email. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to it.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: shows the ticker strip at the top of your screen. It can't read or interact with other apps.
  • Usage access (optional): used only if you turn off "Show on home screen", to check on your phone whether an app or the home screen is in front. This information is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Internet: to fetch prices and to sign in.
  • Foreground service: keeps the strip running while you use other apps.

How we use information

Only to sign you in, to remember your free trial so it's used once per Google account, and to unlock Pro features you've paid for. No advertising, no profiling and no selling or renting of data.

Sharing

We don't share your information with anyone. It's processed by Google (Firebase and Google Play) acting as our service provider, under Google's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Your account stays until you delete it. Delete it anytime in the app under Settings → Delete account, or request deletion here. When you delete your account we remove your Firebase account (name, email and user ID).

We keep only the start date of your free trial, linked to your email, so the free trial can't be repeated. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by Google Firebase. Access to your trial record is limited to your own signed-in account.

Children

Ticker Top is intended for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

Not investment advice

Prices may be delayed and are for information only. Ticker Top doesn't recommend buying or selling anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: managemyfinance.in@gmail.com

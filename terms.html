Estimates, not advice

Interest, maturity, TDS and rate figures are estimates based on what you enter and on published bank rates. They are not financial, tax or legal advice, and the app does not offer deposits, loans or banking services. Please confirm every figure with your bank or a qualified professional before you act on it.

Bank rates

Rates are collected from banks’ published pages and may be out of date or differ from what your branch offers. The rate in your bank’s receipt applies.

Free plan and Premium

The free plan covers your first 2 FDs. Premium is a yearly subscription of ₹399 a year (price shown in Google Play at purchase) that covers every FD and adds rate checks and rate alerts.

Billing and cancellation

Premium is billed through Google Play and renews automatically each year until you cancel. To cancel, open Google Play, go to Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and select Smart FD Tracker. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy.

Your data is stored on your phone. Please keep a backup if it matters to you. See the privacy policy.

About

Developed by Chaitanya Mankar. Smart FD Tracker helps users track Fixed Deposits, maturity dates, interest earnings and financial goals.

About me

Hi, I’m Chaitanya Mankar from Pune, India.

I created Smart FD Tracker because I noticed that many people struggle to track multiple Fixed Deposits across banks, monitor maturity dates, calculate returns accurately and keep their financial records organized.

My interest in personal finance started at a young age. My uncle Shrikrishna Kothawale and my aunt Vasanti Kothawale introduced me to the importance of maintaining financial records, tracking investments, documenting transactions, and staying organized. Watching how they carefully managed and recorded financial information taught me that disciplined record-keeping is one of the foundations of good financial planning.

I was also deeply influenced by my mother, Kalyani Mankar, who taught me that even small amounts saved consistently can make a meaningful difference over time. She showed me that financial success is not always about earning more, but also about valuing every rupee, spending wisely, and building savings through patience and discipline.

These lessons stayed with me throughout my life and inspired me to create Smart FD Tracker. My goal is to build simple and practical tools that help individuals and families stay organized, track their savings and investments and make informed financial decisions with confidence.

I would also like to express my heartfelt gratitude to my mentor, Prof. Abhay K. Abhyankar, whose guidance, encouragement and support have had a profound impact on my life. His belief in learning, discipline, and continuous improvement has been a lasting source of inspiration.

My mission

To make financial tracking simple, reliable, and accessible for everyone.

Through Smart FD Tracker, I aim to help people manage their Fixed Deposits, monitor maturity dates, track earnings, stay organized and develop better financial habits.

Every rupee matters. Every investment deserves to be tracked.

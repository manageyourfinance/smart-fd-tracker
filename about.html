About

Smart FD Tracker helps Indian families keep every fixed deposit, maturity, interest and tax detail in one place.

Families often hold FDs in several banks and in several names. Dates get missed, TDS is a surprise and renewals happen on autopilot. The app puts all of it on one screen and reminds you before it matters.

What we stand for

  • Your data stays yours. It is stored on your phone, with no ads and no tracking.
  • Plain numbers. Amounts in rupees with Indian grouping, dates like 08 Oct 2026, and every figure explained.
  • Honest estimates. The app calculates, your bank confirms.

Questions

Short answers about data, plans and how the numbers work.

Is my FD data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your profiles and FDs are stored on your phone. The app has no server of its own and does not send your details to us.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Install the app and start adding FDs.

What does the free plan cover?

Your first 2 FDs get maturities, interest and TDS, reminders and PDF reports. Premium covers every FD and adds rate checks and rate change alerts.

How is the maturity amount calculated?

For cumulative FDs, interest compounds every quarter. For FDs that pay out interest, the app uses simple interest for each payout period. These are estimates. Your bank’s figures are the final word.

How does TDS work in the app?

Each profile has a TDS setting. Where TDS is deducted, the app takes 10% of the interest and shows it on the FD, in reports and on renewal.

Where do the FD rates come from?

Rates are collected from banks’ published rate pages and reviewed before they are released. The app downloads the latest file when you open it. Always confirm the rate with your bank before you invest.

Does the app move or manage my money?

No. It is a tracker. It does not connect to your bank, hold money or offer deposits or loans.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi and Marathi.

How do I back up my data?

Use Back up in Settings. It saves one file in a private folder of your own Google Drive. You can restore it on a new phone.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play, go to Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Smart FD Tracker. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period.

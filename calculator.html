How this FD calculator works

It uses the formula most Indian banks use for a cumulative FD, where interest is compounded every quarter: maturity amount = P × (1 + r ÷ 4)4t. Read how FD interest is calculated for a worked example.

What it does not cover

  • FDs that pay interest out each month, quarter or year (the app handles these).
  • Different compounding or day counts used by some banks.
  • Your slab rate, since TDS here is a flat 10% of the interest. See how TDS on FD works.
